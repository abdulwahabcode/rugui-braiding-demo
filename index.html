About Us

A salon built on
craft and care

Rugui African Hair Braiding is a neighborhood braiding salon in Oak Park, Illinois, specializing in the protective styles our community loves — from classic box braids and cornrows to Senegalese twists and faux locs.

We believe great braiding is a craft: careful parting, even tension, and respect for the health of your natural hair. Whether it's your first set of braids or your fiftieth, you'll leave our chair feeling confident.
